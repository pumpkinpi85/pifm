HOW TO BROADCAST

        
    
          
  1. Choose your music on the Music page.
    2. 
          
  2. Check your station and frequency on the Station page.
    3. 
          
  3. Return to Broadcast, drag the brass handle out of the OFF AIR detent and release on a frequency.
    4. 
        

        
To end transmission: drag the brass handle into the bottom OFF AIR detent and release, or use Stop Broadcast in the masthead.

      

      

        
Start broadcasting

        
The PumpkinPi flag stays raised at the top of the pole. While OFF AIR (and while previewing a frequency) it shows the black-and-white emblem. When authoritative state confirms ON AIR, the emblem lights orange — same size and position. Drag the brass handle upward from its OFF AIR detent; after release and confirmation, broadcast startup begins.

        
Pause vs Stop Broadcast

        
Pause holds silence on the carrier while staying on air.

        
OFF AIR detent / Stop Broadcast immediately ends FM transmission. Stop Broadcast remains available while starting, after a fault, or when state is unknown.

        
Play / Previous / Next

        
These change the music program. They do not start a broadcast by themselves.

        
Quiet network vs Stop Broadcast

        
Quiet network is an interference troubleshooting tool. It does not stop the FM broadcast.

        
Needs Attention / Possible Transmission

        
If the console shows Needs Attention, State Unknown, or Possible Transmission, press Stop Broadcast, then use System → Fault Recovery if needed.
