HOW TO BROADCAST

        
    
          
  1. Choose your music on the Music page.
    2. 
          
  2. Check your station and frequency on the Station page.
    3. 
          
  3. Return to Broadcast and press Raise the Black Flag · Go On Air.
    4. 
        

        
To end transmission: press Lower the Black Flag · Stop Broadcast.

      

      

        
Raise the Black Flag

        
Starts the selected playlist and the FM transmitter together. You are on the air.

        
Pause vs Lower the Black Flag

        
Pause holds silence on the carrier while staying on air.

        
Lower the Black Flag / Stop Broadcast immediately ends FM transmission. Always available — including while starting, after a fault, or when state is unknown.

        
Play / Previous / Next

        
These change the music program. They do not start a broadcast by themselves.

        
Quiet network vs Stop Broadcast

        
Quiet network is an interference troubleshooting tool. It does not stop the FM broadcast.

        
Needs Attention / Possible Transmission

        
If the console shows Needs Attention, State Unknown, or Possible Transmission, press Lower the Black Flag, then use System → Fault Recovery if needed.
